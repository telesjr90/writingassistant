# LeanCTX rule for Claude

This client rule is subordinate, in order, to `AGENTS.md`, current explicit
owner decisions, and current roadmap/task authority.

Do not use LeanCTX automatically for reads, searches, shell commands,
implementation, documentation, validation, acceptance, or closeout. Ordinary
tasks use the focused direct reads and exact commands authorized by the task.

LeanCTX may run only when the owner explicitly authorizes a context-generation
task. Its output is generated navigation evidence, not product truth, roadmap
authority, implementation proof, or acceptance evidence. Stay within the
authorized input and output scope, and do not inspect or troubleshoot MCP/tool
descriptors during ordinary work.
