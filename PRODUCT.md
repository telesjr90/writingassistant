# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Fiction writers and story creators working in a local project workspace. They need to capture and edit owner-authored material, organize project context, inspect analysis results, and decide explicitly what, if anything, becomes approved project truth.

The primary workflow is moving from owner-authored source material to evidence-backed analysis and review without losing author control or confusing candidates, confidence, queue presence, or model output with canon.

## Product Purpose

The Dramatica-Informed Writing Assistant is a local, analysis-only, owner-controlled writing workspace and analysis tool. It stores and organizes owner-authored prose and project materials, performs bounded structural and continuity analysis, and presents findings as evidence- and provenance-backed candidates for explicit owner review.

Success means the owner can understand possible structural issues, continuity concerns, unresolved questions, and project context; review each candidate truthfully; and make deliberate approval or promotion decisions without the application generating story prose or automatically mutating approved Memory or Canon.

## Brand Personality

Calm, rigorous, trustworthy.

The product voice is direct, precise, and non-performative. It should reduce uncertainty without pretending that analysis, confidence, or model output is authoritative truth.

## Anti-references

- A generic AI chat or co-writer that writes, rewrites, continues, imitates, polishes, expands, outlines, drafts, revises, or improves story prose.
- An opaque automated system that presents confidence, candidates, queue entries, or model output as truth or silently promotes them.
- A dense generic SaaS administration dashboard made from repetitive cards, banners, and metadata with no clear primary task.
- Decorative or playful presentation that obscures evidence, provenance, risk, candidate status, or owner checkpoints.
- Interfaces that make unavailable, blocked, review-only, or not-yet-implemented behavior appear actionable.

## Design Principles

1. Truthful affordances over apparent capability.
2. Owner authority at every consequential transition.
3. Evidence and provenance before confidence.
4. Task-focused progressive disclosure instead of repeated metadata and safety text.
5. Accessible, responsive, and consistent interaction without erasing candidate, approval, provenance, or canon distinctions.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No formal WCAG conformance claim has yet been accepted; later responsive, accessibility, keyboard, zoom, screen-reader, and validation tasks own that evidence.

Design and implementation must support semantic headings, keyboard operation, visible focus, adequate text contrast, non-color-only status communication, practical target sizes, reduced-motion preferences, screen-reader clarity, browser zoom, and responsive containment from 320 through 1440 CSS pixels.

Accessibility must preserve truthful state distinctions, owner authority, evidence visibility, and the separation between candidates and approved project truth.
